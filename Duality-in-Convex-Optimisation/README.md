# Convex Optimisation & Lagrangian Duality

A solo research essay exploring convex optimisation theory and the theory of Lagrangian duality.

## Overview

This essay introduces the foundations of convex optimisation, develops the theory of Lagrangian duality, and examines the KKT conditions. The focus is on clear exposition of theory with worked examples, bridging abstract mathematical concepts with practical intuition.
